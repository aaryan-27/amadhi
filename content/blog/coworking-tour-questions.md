Every coworking tour is scheduled at a quiet hour, starts in the nicest corner and ends at the coffee machine. That is not dishonest, it is sales. Your job is to find out what the space is like on a Tuesday afternoon when it's full.

A good operator answers all of this without hesitating. Hesitation is itself information.

## Before you go

- Go at the busiest time you can — **mid-week, mid-afternoon**. A Monday 10am tour tells you nothing.
- Bring whoever will actually sit there. They notice things founders don't.
- Run a speed test on their guest Wi-Fi while you walk around.

## About the space

1. **How many desks are on this floor, and how many are taken?** A half-empty floor is quiet now and may not be in three months.
2. **Can I see the actual desks I'd get?** Not a similar area — the specific seats.
3. **How loud does it get?** Then judge for yourself; open floors with hard surfaces carry sound.
4. **Where do people take calls?** If the answer is "at their desk", expect noise. Count the booths.
5. **What's above, below and next door?** A gym or a restaurant floor changes the day.
6. **What happens when the power goes?** Backup for the whole floor, or lights and lifts only? Does air conditioning stay on?

## About the internet

7. **Is bandwidth shared or dedicated, and what is the committed rate?**
8. **Is there a second line from a different provider**, and does it fail over automatically?
9. **Who fixes it at 4pm on a Friday, and how fast?**

For teams that live on video calls this matters more than the desk rate.

## About meeting rooms

10. **How many hours are included per seat per month, and do they roll over?**
11. **What do extra hours cost?**
12. **How far ahead do rooms get booked?** Ask to see next week's calendar. Fully booked means you will struggle.
13. **Is there a room that holds your largest regular meeting?**

## About the money

14. **What is the all-in monthly cost for my seat count?** Itemised.
15. **What is not included?** Parking, printing, lockers, after-hours air conditioning, guest passes.
16. **How much parking, and at what cost?** In NCR this is a real constraint, not a detail.
17. **What is the escalation, and does it apply to the whole invoice?**
18. **What is the deposit, when is it refunded, and what can be deducted?**

## About the exit

19. **What is the lock-in, and separately, what is the notice period?**
20. **Can I reduce seats mid-term?**
21. **What does it cost to add four seats in six months — and can you accommodate them on this floor?**

That last one matters. Growing teams that have to split across two floors, or move buildings, lose more than they saved by negotiating hard on the rate.

## After the tour

Ask for the itemised quote in writing and compare it against one other space on total monthly cost, not the headline. Then visit your shortlist once more, unannounced if you can, at the same busy hour.

## Frequently asked questions

### What should I ask when visiting a coworking space?

Cover four areas: the space itself (occupancy, noise, the actual desks you'd get, power backup), the internet (dedicated or shared, backup line, support response), meeting rooms (included hours, cost beyond, how heavily booked), and commercial terms (all-in cost, exclusions, deposit, lock-in, notice, cost of adding seats).

### When is the best time to tour a coworking space?

Mid-week, mid-afternoon, when the floor is at its busiest. Tours are usually offered at quiet times, which tells you little about noise, meeting-room availability or parking pressure.

### What are the hidden costs in coworking?

Most commonly meeting-room hours beyond the included allowance, parking, printing, lockers, after-hours air conditioning, guest passes and annual escalation. Ask for an itemised quote so two options can be compared on total monthly cost.

### How do I judge the internet quality?

Ask whether bandwidth is dedicated or shared, what rate is committed, whether a second provider fails over automatically, and how quickly it is fixed when it breaks. Run a speed test yourself while touring, at a busy hour.

### Should I visit more than once?

Yes, ideally at a busy hour and, if possible, unannounced. A second visit is the cheapest insurance against signing for a floor that turns out noisier or fuller than it looked.

### What should I get in writing before signing?

An itemised monthly quote showing inclusions and exclusions, the deposit amount and refund terms, lock-in and notice separately stated, escalation, and the rate for additional seats.
