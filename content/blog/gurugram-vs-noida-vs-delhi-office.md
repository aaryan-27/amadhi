Delhi NCR is not one office market. It is three, with different price levels, different commutes and — often overlooked — **three different GST jurisdictions**. Gurugram sits in Haryana, Noida in Uttar Pradesh, Delhi is its own.

Broadly: **Delhi's central business districts are the most expensive, Gurugram spans the widest range, and Noida is generally the most affordable**. Rather than print figures that go stale, we publish live medians by micro-market — see [Gurugram](/coworking-space/gurugram), [Noida](/coworking-space/noida) and [Delhi](/coworking-space/delhi), each broken down by area.

## Gurugram

The deepest supply in NCR, and the widest price spread. Cyber City and Golf Course Road sit at the premium end; Udyog Vihar, Sohna Road and the Golf Course Extension offer materially cheaper desks within a short drive.

**Suits:** corporates, GCCs, anyone whose team already lives on the southern side of NCR, and companies that want many operators to choose between in one micro-market.

**Watch:** traffic between micro-markets is worse than the map suggests, and a "20-minute" move can be an hour at 6pm. Power and water infrastructure varies sharply by building — ask about backup specifically.

## Noida

Generally the most affordable of the three, with strong metro coverage along the Blue and Aqua lines. Sectors 62, 63 and 16 have dense workspace supply; the Expressway sectors are newer and often better built.

**Suits:** engineering and IT teams, cost-conscious scale-ups, anyone with a team living in east Delhi, Ghaziabad or Noida itself.

**Watch:** sector numbers are not intuitive — 62 and 16 are far apart. Check the specific sector against where your team lives before deciding.

## Delhi

Connaught Place and the central districts carry the highest prices in NCR and the best metro access. South Delhi — Saket, Nehru Place, Okhla — is cheaper and still central. Mohan Cooperative and the Okhla industrial belt are cheaper again.

**Suits:** businesses that need a central address for clients or government work, teams spread across NCR who need a genuinely central meeting point, and anyone who values metro access above parking.

**Watch:** parking is the binding constraint in central Delhi, and often expensive or simply unavailable. If your team drives, price it in before you fall in love with the address.

## The three things that actually decide it

**1. Where your team lives.** This outweighs everything else. An office that adds forty minutes each way to most people's commute costs you in attrition and attendance far more than it saves in rent. Map your team's home addresses before you shortlist buildings.

**2. GST jurisdiction.** Your registered place of business determines the state you register in. If you have operations, warehousing or client contracts tied to a particular state, that has consequences worth checking with your accountant before signing.

**3. Client geography.** If clients visit weekly, being near them is worth paying for. If nobody visits, you are buying a commute, not an address — and should optimise accordingly.

## A reasonable way to decide

1. Plot where your team lives and find the centre of gravity.
2. Pick the two or three micro-markets within a reasonable commute of it.
3. Compare current medians for those areas on our city pages.
4. Shortlist in the cheaper of them first — the premium micro-market should have to justify itself.
5. Confirm the GST implication with your accountant before signing.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which is cheaper for office space: Gurugram, Noida or Delhi?

Noida is generally the most affordable, Delhi's central districts the most expensive, and Gurugram covers the widest range — its cheaper micro-markets compete with Noida while Cyber City and Golf Course Road sit near the top. Current medians by area are on our city pages.

### Does it matter which city in NCR I register my office in?

Yes. GST is state-wise, so Gurugram registers you in Haryana, Noida in Uttar Pradesh and Delhi in Delhi. If your operations or contracts are tied to a state, confirm the implications with your accountant before choosing.

### Which NCR location has the best metro connectivity?

Central Delhi has the densest network, and Noida is well served by the Blue and Aqua lines. Gurugram's coverage is thinner and concentrated along the Rapid Metro corridor near Cyber City, so more people drive.

### Where should a startup set up in Delhi NCR?

Usually wherever the founding team and early hires already live, in the cheapest micro-market within a sensible commute. Commute drives attendance and retention more than the address does.

### Is Gurugram or Noida better for IT and engineering teams?

Both work. Noida tends to be cheaper with better metro coverage; Gurugram has deeper supply and more operators to choose from. The deciding factor is usually where your engineers live.

### How much does parking matter in NCR?

A great deal, and it varies sharply. Central Delhi is the most constrained, Gurugram assumes driving, and Noida is generally the easiest. Ask how many bays are included and what extra ones cost before signing.
